About Just 4 Kids

Filomena Cauli MSN, APRN, CPNP-PC

Experienced & Compassionate Pediatric Care

With more than three decades of nursing experience and a deep commitment to children’s health, our provider brings both expertise and heart to every family we serve. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Texas Medical Branch in 1990 and later completed her Master of Science in Nursing at the University of Texas Health Science Center in 2007. She is licensed by the Texas Board of Nursing and is certified as an Advanced Pediatric Nurse Practitioner and Certified Pediatric Nurse.

Her career includes years of dedicated service at Park Plaza Pediatrics (2008–2016) and a decade of caring for families here at Just4Kids Pediatrics, where she continues to provide compassionate, evidence-based care.

Her professional mission is simple and powerful :
  • Promote health and prevent disease
  • Deliver high-quality, personalized pediatric care
  • Support and educate families as a whole
  • Strengthen the well-being of children throughout our community

Beyond the clinic, she enjoys spending time with her family, singing, dancing, Zumba, playing piano (and sometimes guitar), and reading — passions that bring joy and balance to her life.

At Just4Kids Pediatrics, we warmly welcome you and your family. Our goal is to make every visit comfortable, friendly, and centered around the health and happiness of your child.

Healthy Children Today, Strong and Happy Tomorrow Always
