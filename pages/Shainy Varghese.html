About Just 4 Kids

Dr. Shainy Varghese, PhD, APRN, CPNP-PC, CGNC

Caring for kids. Supporting families. Leading with heart and expertise.

Dr. Shainy Varghese is a Pediatric Nurse Practitioner and Professor at the University of Houston College of Nursing. With a Ph.D. in Nursing and a dissertation focused on Telehealth, she brings deep clinical knowledge and a passion for innovation to every child she cares for.

She has been practicing as a Pediatric Nurse Practitioner since 2004 and opened a nurse-managed clinic in Stafford in 2010 to serve underserved children in the community. She also played a key role in launching the UH College of Nursing Health Clinic to support homeless and underserved families in Midtown Houston.

Her research focuses on :
  • Telehealth
  • Vitamin D Deficiency in Children
  • Gaming Addiction in Children

Dr. Varghese is a published researcher, a Global Nurse Consultant, and a Fulbright Specialist (2022–2025). She has presented her work at local, national, and international conferences and was honored as one of the Houston Chronicle’s Top 150 Nurses and a Good Samaritan Foundation Bronze Medal recipient.

Outside of her professional work, she enjoys cooking, traveling, listening to music, and spending time with her family.

We’re proud to have Dr. Varghese leading our mission of compassionate, evidence-based pediatric care.
