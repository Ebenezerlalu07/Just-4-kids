Finding Inspiration in Every Turn

Just 4 Kids Pediatric Clinic is a nurse practitioner-owned and operated clinic founded by Dr. Shainy Varghese in 2010. With certified nurse practitioners, we provide well-child and sick visits, in-house labs, and immunizations. Our mission is to provide quality, accessible, evidence-based medical care in a family-centered environment. As pediatric primary care providers, we take responsibility for children's health and well-being, placing children at the center of care.

Child Safety always comes first (Our Policies)

Complete your child's required intake information before arrival whenever possible. Mobile-friendly digital forms help reduce check-in time and allow our team to review important medical information before the scheduled visit.

1
Appointments

Patient visits to Just 4 Kids Pediatric Clinic are by appointment. For scheduling purposes, every sibling needs an appointment to be seen by the provider. Appointments must be confirmed within 24 hours.

2
Cancellation Policy

All cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made 24 hours in advance. If you expect to arrive more than 15 minutes late, please call the office. You may be asked to reschedule your appointment.

3
No-show Policy

If you do not call to notify us of a cancellation or delay, the appointment may be considered a no-show. After three no-shows, a $25 fee may apply.

4
Parental Consent for Care Policy

All adults involved in the care of the child are encouraged to be part of office visits. Any adult who is not the child's parent must have written permission from the parent to bring the child in for medical care.
